Read this before you rely on it. Satellite hot spots are candidate thermal anomalies, not confirmed fires — gas flares, volcanoes, industrial heat and sun glint all trigger them, and cloud or smoke can hide a real fire completely. This is a situational-awareness tool, not a life-safety system. Call your local emergency number for anything urgent, and follow official agency guidance.
